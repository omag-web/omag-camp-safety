What Ranger always does (can't be edited here)
These rules are built into Ranger's code so they can't be removed by accident:
  • Says it's an AI assistant provided by OMAG, not OMAG staff and not a lawyer.
  • Searches the statute library and reads the actual sections before answering legal questions.
  • Cites only sections it actually read; unverified citations are removed automatically.
  • Never presents itself as legal counsel.

Sample documents

OMAG's example plans, policies and forms. When a member asks Ranger to write that kind of document, Ranger reads the matching sample as a guide to what it should cover, then writes the member's document from scratch for their camp. It never copies a sample's wording, names or numbers. Only samples that are switched on are used.

What makes a good sample
  • Upload the Word file as it is. No fields, tags or questions are needed.
  • One good, complete example per kind of document is plenty: an emergency action plan, a water safety plan, a medication procedure, and so on.
  • Take out real names, phone numbers and addresses first. Ranger won't copy them, but there's no reason to store them.
  • Write a clear one-line description. It's how Ranger decides which sample matches a request, e.g. “Water safety plan: swim tests, lifeguards, ratios, buddy checks”.
  • A sample can have a separate version for one program type, such as a recreational campground version of the emergency action plan. Members of that type get that version; everyone else gets the one for every program type.

What the buttons do

            
Access: choose what they can see. Reports = Analytics. Content = Instructions, OMAG documents, sample documents, statute library and answer checks. Conversations and messages = members’ conversations and “Talk to OMAG” notes. Settings = limits, AI model, welcome screen, privacy notice and email lists. Admins = this page. At least one admin always keeps Admins.

            
Resend invite: for someone who hasn't signed in yet, or whose 7 days ran out. Sends a new temporary password.

            
Reset password: for someone who's locked out. Emails them a code; they use "Forgot password?" on the sign-in page.

            
Turn off: they can't sign in, and are signed out within a minute. Reversible.

            
Remove: deletes their account. To add them back, invite them again.

            
Reset two-step: for someone who lost or replaced their phone. It only appears next to people whose two-step is On; someone showing Off either hasn't set it up yet or set it up before this column could see it; it changes to On the next time they sign in with their code. After a reset they set it up again with the new phone at their next sign-in (or, if two-step isn't required, sign in with just their password).

            
You can't turn off or remove yourself, or take Admins away from yourself.
